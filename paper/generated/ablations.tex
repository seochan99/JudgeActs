The slot preference could be induced by our interface rather than by the judge. We ran three post-hoc ablations with Qwen, leaving the frozen main results unchanged.

\textit{Full rotation.} Adding the fourth rotation for the 153 four-image pools places every image in every slot once. Slot shares are then A 51.0\%, B 20.8\%, C 14.7\%, D 13.6\% (uniform 25.0\%); unanimity over four orders keeps 28.8\% of these pools with regret 0.095, and the order-plurality policy has gain 0.062 [0.031, 0.092].

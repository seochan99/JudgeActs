A small VLM judge that chooses which image to return improves alignment over random choice only slightly (0.039), and less than a CLIP similarity scorer. Its choices depend strongly on presentation order: it picks the first-listed image in 48.8\% of calls. Agreement across orders separates prompts where the judge adds value from prompts where it falls below chance, whereas agreement with a weaker judge does not. Neither selector avoids stereotyped images.

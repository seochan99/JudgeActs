Qwen's point estimate of gain is positive in 7 of 10 country groups (Appendix Figure~\ref{fig:country}), ranging from 0.107 in Canada to -0.018 in India. Regret is highest in Japan (0.275, pointwise 95\% CI [0.171, 0.387]), where random choice also has the highest regret (0.266). With 30 prompts per group, these intervals are wide and overlap; country labels describe dataset contexts, not cultural preferences, and the analysis is not powered to rank groups.

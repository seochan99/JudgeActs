Figure~\ref{fig:cultural} compares the cultural-error ratings of Qwen's choices with random choice on the same pools. Qwen lowers missing explicit expectations by 0.056 [-0.084, -0.026] and missing implicit expectations by 0.034 [-0.057, -0.009], and both reductions survive a Bonferroni adjustment across the three error types. It raises the stereotype rating from 0.125 to 0.148 (difference 0.023, pointwise 95\% CI [0.002, 0.045]); this interval includes zero after adjustment ([-0.002, 0.051]), so we treat it as exploratory. On the pools where stereotype ratings differ across candidates, the difference is 0.036 [0.004, 0.068] (Appendix Table~\ref{tab:decomposition}). An alignment gain therefore need not carry over to every cultural outcome.

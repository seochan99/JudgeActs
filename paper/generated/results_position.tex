Both judges favor early slots (Figure~\ref{fig:results}c). Qwen picks slot A in 48.8\% of its 900 calls, where uniform choice given the pool sizes would give 27.8\% ($\chi^2_{3}=219$, $p<10^{-10}$); Smol places 93.3\% of its choices in slots A or B. Consistent with this, Qwen's choice changes across the three orders for 59.7\% of prompts. Figure~\ref{fig:hero} shows the extreme case: the judge returns whatever image is shown first.

The judge still responds to content. Canonical order places one generator first in most pools, so we pool all three rotations, which spreads each generator across slots: Qwen then chooses images from the generator with the highest mean human rating in 57.9\% of calls, against 27.2\% under uniform choice (Appendix Table~\ref{tab:generators}). Its choices mix a content signal with a strong slot preference.

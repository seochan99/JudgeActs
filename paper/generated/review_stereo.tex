Where does the stereotype increase come from? Most of it traces to one generator: Qwen returns SD-3.5-Large images in 41.3\% of prompts against 27.6\% under uniform choice, and these images carry the highest stereotype ratings (Appendix Table~\ref{tab:extras-stereo}). The increase is also larger when several candidates tie for the best alignment (0.038 [0.004, 0.073]) than when one candidate is clearly best (0.014 [$-$0.012, 0.040]). Aggregating across orders leaves no detectable increase: the stereotype difference is 0.007 [$-$0.013, 0.027] for voting over the three orders and 0.010 [$-$0.019, 0.039] on unanimous prompts, whereas on the prompts unanimity rejects it is 0.032 [0.003, 0.063]. Both aggregated policies still reduce missing explicit expectations ($-$0.092 and $-$0.174).
